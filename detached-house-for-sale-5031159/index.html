Sherry FitzGerald is truly delighted to bring 14 Weston Court to the market.

A truly impressive five-bedroom detached family residence, ideally positioned at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in a mature and highly regarded residential setting in Lucan.

Presented in excellent condition throughout, this striking red-brick, double-fronted home has been thoughtfully extended to the rear, creating an abundance of versatile living, family and entertaining space.

From the moment you approach the property, it is clear that this is a substantial and distinctive family home. The generous gravel driveway provides excellent off-street parking for up to five cars, while the exceptionally private rear garden, mature planting and expansive footprint further enhance the appeal of this wonderful property.

The accommodation is particularly well suited to modern family life, with generous reception rooms, a large open-plan kitchen and family area, dedicated home office, playroom, sunroom, utility room and five bedrooms, dressing room, together with additional converted attic accommodation.

On entering through the tiled storm porch, you are welcomed into a spacious and bright entrance hall with tiled flooring. To the right is a well-appointed playroom, a wonderfully versatile room which could equally lend itself to use as a children's room, office, hobby room or additional reception space.

Further along the hall is the formal living room, featuring solid timber flooring, a gas open fire with granite inset and attractive timber surround, ceiling coving and a large box-bay window overlooking the front driveway. Double doors open directly into the impressive open-plan kitchen, dining and family area, creating an excellent flow between the formal and informal living spaces.

A guest WC, complete with wash hand basin, is conveniently positioned beneath the stairs. At the end of the hall is a separate home office, perfectly positioned away from the main living areas and ideal for those working remotely or running a business from home.

The heart of this wonderful home is undoubtedly the expansive rear extension, where three distinct family areas have been seamlessly brought together to create an exceptional open-plan living environment. The family recreation area provides a relaxed setting with TV point and excellent space for everyday family life. At the centre is the well-appointed kitchen, incorporating a breakfast bar and an excellent range of storage units. The kitchen is equipped with an integrated oven, integrated dishwasher, stainless-steel sink, integrated microwave, electric hob and overhead extractor fan. A large picture window overlooks the impressive rear garden, allowing natural light to flood the space.

Adjoining the kitchen is a dedicated dining area, with double doors opening directly onto the extensive side patio and rear garden, making this an ideal space for entertaining and summer dining. A spacious utility room is located just off the kitchen and benefits from a glazed single door providing convenient access to the rear patio and garden.

From the family living area, double doors open into a bright and versatile sunroom.

Upstairs, a carpeted staircase leads to a spacious landing. The principal bedroom suite is an exceptional space and has been cleverly reconfigured by incorporating what was originally a sixth bedroom. The result is a superbly proportioned bedroom with an impressive open-plan dressing area, extensive floor-to-ceiling storage and a box-bay window. An ensuite bathroom completes the suite and features a corner bath with overhead shower, WC and wash hand basin.

Bedroom two is a generous double bedroom positioned to the rear, featuring laminate flooring, ceiling coving and a centre rose. Bedroom three is another excellent double bedroom, again benefiting from laminate flooring, ceiling coving and a centre rose. Bedroom four, positioned to the rear, is a further generously proportioned bedroom with laminate flooring, ceiling coving, centre rose and built-in wardrobes. Bedroom five, positioned to the front, is a spacious single bedroom with built-in wardrobes and offers excellent flexibility as a child's bedroom, guest room or additional study space. A spacious family bathroom is positioned between Bedrooms four and five and features a walk-in shower, wash hand basin and WC.

From the landing, a spiral staircase leads to the converted attic, providing yet another valuable layer of accommodation. The attic landing incorporates useful under-eaves storage, while to the opposite side is an additional room which could provide guest accommodation, teenage space or another private retreat. This room benefits from its own ensuite, alcove storage and solid timber flooring.

If the generous accommodation within does not impress, the gardens certainly will. Positioned at the end of the cul-de-sac, the property occupies a particularly generous site which fans out towards the rear, creating an expansive and exceptionally private garden.

The rear garden is a wonderful space for family life and entertaining, offering ample room for summer parties, barbecues and family gatherings. A large patio area to the right provides the perfect setting for alfresco dining, while the beautifully maintained lawn extends across much of the garden. Gravelled areas, landscaped walkways, patio space and mature lawn combine to create a highly attractive and easily enjoyed outdoor environment.

A substantial block-built shed provides excellent additional storage and could, subject to the necessary permissions, offer potential for conversion to another use. The garden is enclosed by an abundance of mature trees, specimen planting and established shrubs, creating a wonderful sense of privacy and seclusion.

The property enjoys a peaceful position at the end of Weston Court while remaining within easy reach of the many amenities Lucan has to offer. The area provides an excellent choice of schools, shops, cafés, restaurants and recreational facilities, while transport links provide convenient access to Dublin City Centre and surrounding areas.

14 Weston Court is a truly exceptional family home which combines generous proportions, superb versatility and beautifully maintained outdoor space. The combination of five bedrooms, extensive reception accommodation, a large rear extension, converted attic, multiple home-working options and an impressive private garden makes this a particularly special property.

Whether catering for a growing family, accommodating extended family or simply providing the space required for modern family life, this wonderful home offers flexibility in abundance. Properties of this size, quality and character rarely come to the market in Lucan and early viewing is highly recommended.
